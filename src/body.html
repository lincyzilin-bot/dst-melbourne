📖 HOW TO PLAY

Watch the video, or read the quick guide below.

🎯 GOAL
Explore to raise lifetime % (saved forever). Survive to Day 4 → 💧 Wetland, Day 5 → 🚜 Farm, Day 7 → 🏚️ Ruins. Dying ends the run but keeps exploration.
🕹️ CONTROLS
WASD/arrows walk · click = walk there · E chop/mine/pick/offer · Space attack · Q eat selected food · C light campfire · B lay bridge at water
❤️🍖🧠 THE THREE BARS
🍖 Hunger always drains — at 0 you starve (-HP). Eat berries (+12), wheat (+14), morsel (+16), meat (+20), petals (+5).
🧠 Sanity falls every night spent in the dark — pick 🌸 flowers (+5), craft a 🌼 garland, or stand near 🔥 fire.
❤️ Health regrows while hunger is above 60. Starving, darkness, spider bites (-8) and angry possums (-6) all hurt.
🛠️ WHAT TO CRAFT (bottom bar)
🪓 Axe (2 log + 1 stone) — chop trees, get logs.
⛏️ Pickaxe (1 log + 2 stone) — mine rubble AND the only way to harvest 🌾 wheat.
🗡️ Spear (3 log + 1 stone) — 34 damage instead of 10.
🔥 Fire kit (3 log + 1 stone) — press C, restores sanity, blocks night damage.
🌼 Garland (6 petals) — slows night sanity loss.
🌉 Bridge kit (4 log + 2 stone) — press B at water; every river and the moat needs your own bridge.
🕷️ HOW TO KILL SPIDERS
They spawn at night (up to 4), chase you, and bite for -8 HP. Craft a spear first: 2 spear hits kill one (60 HP). No spear? Punching works but takes 6 hits — light a fire and fight beside it so your sanity survives too. Befriended 🦡 possums fight spiders for you. Leftover spiders fade at dawn. Loot: 🍖 meat (+20 hunger).
🐇🦡 RABBITS & POSSUMS
🐇 Rabbits roam grassland by day and flee you — too fast to grab (E won't work), hunt with Space for 🍗 morsel.
🦡 Park possums: select any food/flowers, press E twice to befriend (❤️ follows + fights spiders). Attack one and it turns ❗ enemy for good.
🌊 WATER & 🌙 NIGHT
Moat + rivers block you (watch the hint text). Ponds: walk around. Night: get to firelight — out in the dark you lose sanity AND health.
